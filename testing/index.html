🎯 The 14 Simple Daily Tests

Pick today's test, follow the 3 quick steps, and tap "Mark Done":

DAY 1 Onboarding & Master PIN Setup
  • Install Kunjika from the Google Play Beta link and open it.
  • Swipe through the onboarding screens explaining offline security.
  • Set a memorable 4 to 8-digit Master PIN and confirm it.
  • Link your Fingerprint or Face unlock when prompted.
✓ Expected: Vault unlocks smoothly with your biometric or PIN.
DAY 2 Generate a Strong Random Password
  • Tap the Generator tab at the bottom.
  • Select Password mode and drag the length slider to 16–20 characters.
  • Toggle symbols and numbers; notice the real-time strength meter and crack-time update.
  • Tap Copy to verify copying works.
✓ Expected: Generates high-entropy passwords with instant strength rating.
DAY 3 Try Word Passphrases & Numeric PINs
  • In the Generator tab, tap Passphrase (word-based like correct-horse-battery-staple).
  • Adjust the word count slider to 4 or 5 words.
  • Switch to PIN mode and generate a 6-digit banking passcode.
  • Scroll down to the Generation History card to view your past generated items.
✓ Expected: Generates memorable word phrases and logs past passwords.
DAY 4 Save Your First Account to the Vault
  • Tap the Vault tab and tap the floating `+` (Add) button.
  • Enter Title (e.g. My Email) and your username.
  • Tap the dice icon to auto-generate a secure password into the field.
  • Select category Personal and tap Save.
✓ Expected: Account is safely encrypted and displayed in your vault list.
DAY 5 Search & Category Filters
  • Add 2 more sample accounts under different categories (e.g. Work and Finance).
  • Tap the category filter chips at the top (Work, Finance) to view filtered lists.
  • Use the search bar at the top to type an account name and test instant search.
✓ Expected: Instant search and filtering without any lag.
DAY 6 Built-In 2FA Authenticator (TOTP)
  • Open any account in your Vault and tap the pencil icon to edit.
  • In the TOTP Secret field, paste test secret JBSWY3DPEHPK3PXP and save.
  • Tap the account card to view the rotating 6-digit code and 30s countdown ring.
  • Tap the 6-digit code to copy it to your clipboard.
✓ Expected: Live rotating 2FA codes without needing Google Authenticator.
DAY 7 Enable Android Autofill
  • Go to the ⚙️ Settings tab and tap Autofill Service.
  • Select Kunjika as your default autofill provider in Android Settings.
  • Open Chrome or any browser login form, tap a password box.
  • Tap Kunjika's prompt, unlock with fingerprint, and watch it fill your login.
✓ Expected: One-tap biometric autofill across apps and websites.
DAY 8 Web Drop: Beam a Password to PC
  • On your computer (Chrome, Edge, Brave), open https://dwinsi.github.io/kunjika/web-companion/.
  • On your phone, open any saved vault item and tap ⚡ Web Drop.
  • Scan the QR code on your computer screen with your phone camera.
  • Confirm matching 6-digit code, approve with fingerprint, and press Ctrl+V on your laptop!
✓ Expected: Password beams to PC clipboard via Bluetooth with zero internet.
DAY 9 The Airplane Mode (100% Offline) Test
  • Turn on Airplane Mode on your phone (disable Wi-Fi and Mobile Data).
  • Open Kunjika, unlock with your fingerprint, and generate a new password.
  • Add or edit an account in your Vault.
✓ Expected: Operates 100% normally without any network error or delay.
DAY 10 Screen Privacy Protection (Anti-Spyware)
  • Open Kunjika to your Vault.
  • Try taking a screenshot using hardware keys (Power + Volume Down).
  • Swipe up into Android's Recent Apps / Task Switcher.
✓ Expected: Screenshot is blocked and recent apps preview is blanked.
DAY 11 30-Second Clipboard Auto-Clear
  • Open any saved account in your vault and tap Copy Password.
  • Paste it into a text note to verify it copied.
  • Wait 30–45 seconds without copying anything else.
  • Try pasting again in the text note.
✓ Expected: Clipboard is cleared automatically to prevent app snooping.
DAY 12 Run a Security Health Audit
  • Tap the 🛡️ Security tab at the bottom.
  • Review the Vault Hygiene card (weak, reused, or aging passwords).
  • Review the Device Environment Audit (Root detection & hardware key attestation).
✓ Expected: Comprehensive security audit of passwords and phone integrity.
DAY 13 Theme Switch & Emergency Recovery Kit
  • Go to ⚙️ Settings and toggle between Obsidian Gold and Platinum Silver themes.
  • Tap Emergency Recovery Kit and authenticate with fingerprint.
  • Inspect the generated offline recovery document.
✓ Expected: Instant theme toggle and printable offline backup generation.
DAY 14 Submit Feedback & Keep Installed
  • Open Kunjika's page on the Google Play Store app.
  • Scroll down to Private feedback to developer.
  • Write a quick 1–2 line review of your test experience and tap Submit.
  • Keep Kunjika installed on your phone until official public launch!
✓ Expected: Private feedback recorded for Google Play production review.

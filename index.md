# 🏰 KeyFortress

### *Military-Grade, Offline-First Password Vault & Generator for Android*

KeyFortress is a zero-network, ultra-secure Password Manager. It provides a "Sovereign" security model where the user has 100% ownership of their data with cryptographic proofs of integrity.

[**View Documentation**](./docs/) | [**Privacy Policy**](./PRIVACY_POLICY.html)

---

## 🛡️ Security Highlights

- **🚫 Zero Network**: No `INTERNET` permission. Data never leaves your device.
- **🔐 Double Encryption**: AES-256-GCM (KeyStore) + SQLCipher (Database).
- **🧱 Blockchain Audit Log**: A hardware-signed ledger of every vault action.
- **📡 Air-Gapped Sync**: Encrypted QR-based transfer between devices.
- **💎 Hardware-Backed**: Master keys are stored in the device's TEE/HSM.

---

## ✨ Features

- **🎯 Intelligent Generator**: Passwords, Passphrases (Diceware), and PINs.
- **🗳️ Encrypted Vault**: Full management of credentials and secure notes.
- **⏰ TOTP Authenticator**: Built-in 2FA support with hardware-protected secrets.
- **🪄 Native Autofill**: Seamless credential entry in apps and websites.

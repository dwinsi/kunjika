Privacy First & Always

Plain-Language Privacy: What 'Zero-Network' Actually Means

We believe privacy should be mathematically verifiable, not just promised in marketing copy. Here is the exact truth about how your data is protected.

🛡️

Kernel-Level Zero-Internet Guarantee

In Android, an app cannot send or receive internet packets without declaring android.permission.INTERNET. Kunjika explicitly removes this permission from its binary manifest (tools:node="remove"). The Android operating system kernel refuses to allocate network sockets to Kunjika. It is physically impossible for the app to connect to the internet.

🚫 Zero Telemetry & Trackers

No Google Analytics, no Firebase, no Facebook SDK, no advertising IDs, no diagnostic crash uploaders. Kunjika collects, logs, and transmits exactly 0 bytes of user data.

🏢 Zero Cloud Accounts

No email address required, no username to create, no central password reset server. There is no cloud database or honeypot that hackers can breach to reach your passwords.

📱 100% On-Device Vault

All passwords, notes, and 2FA keys reside exclusively inside your phone's encrypted flash memory, protected by hardware silicon ciphers and an encrypted SQLCipher database.

📋 Transparent Breakdown of Device Permissions

USE_BIOMETRIC Authorizes vault access and cryptographically unwraps your Master PIN using the device's hardware KeyStore (TEE / StrongBox). Plaintext PIN is never persisted to disk.
CAMERA Used exclusively for scanning local QR codes (adding 2FA authenticator secret keys and Web Drop pairing codes). Camera frames are processed strictly in volatile RAM via CameraX and ML Kit; no photos or videos are ever saved to disk or transmitted.
BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE / CONNECT Used strictly during an active Web Drop session to broadcast a localized BLE peripheral service and beam encrypted credentials directly to your laptop's browser over local radio waves. Deactivates immediately after transmission. Never used for beaconing, location tracking, or telemetry.
Storage Access Framework Kunjika does not request broad file access. When you choose to export an encrypted .kunjika backup, it uses the official Android system file picker (SAF) to let you pick a USB drive, SD card, or local folder.

⚖️ Clear & Grounded Security Boundaries

• Hardware Backing: KeyStore hardware protection depends on your device hardware (TEE vs StrongBox).
• Access Gate: The Master PIN is an access gate in the application UI, while the database is encrypted with a random passphrase wrapped by Android KeyStore.
• Local Data Sharing: When you use Web Drop, QR sync, clipboard copy, Android Autofill, or file export, selected credentials leave Kunjika into the receiving application or device.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Kunjika's security, offline model, and setup.

If Kunjika is 100% offline, what happens if I lose, break, or upgrade my phone? ▼
Kunjika's portable .kunjika backup files are encrypted with your custom Backup Passphrase using bank-grade AES-256-GCM + PBKDF2. When you restore your backup on a new or replacement phone, Kunjika decrypts the portable file using your passphrase and automatically re-encrypts all entries with the new phone's physical Android Keystore hardware chip (TEE/HSM)—ensuring smooth migration without decryption errors.
How do I save my backup file to a USB flash drive or SD card? ▼
Go to Settings → Encrypted Offline Backup → Save File. Kunjika connects directly to Android's official Storage Access Framework (SAF) file picker, allowing you to choose any destination—like a USB-C flash drive, external SD card, or local download folder—completely offline without Kunjika ever needing internet permission.
Why does opening the Vault require BOTH my fingerprint AND Master PIN? ▼
For maximum security, Kunjika's Vault implements True Dual-Factor Authentication (2FA) at unlock. Step 1 requires your physical Fingerprint / Biometric scan, and Step 2 requires your Master PIN. Even if someone shoulder-surfs your PIN or attempts to use your fingerprint while you sleep, neither factor alone can open your vault.
What are "High-Security Entries" and how do they protect my sensitive accounts? ▼
You can mark critical accounts (like Banking, Crypto Wallets, or Primary Email) as "High Security". Even when your vault is unlocked, viewing, revealing, or copying a High-Security password or 2FA code requires an additional Biometric Step-Up re-verification (fingerprint scan), preventing a phone-snatcher from accessing your most valuable credentials.
What is the Anti-Coercion Duress PIN and how does the Decoy Vault work? ▼
If you are forced or threatened to unlock Kunjika, entering your pre-configured secret Duress PIN instead of your real Master PIN instantly opens a realistic Decoy Vault populated with dummy accounts (like Netflix, Spotify, or School Portal)—completely concealing your real encrypted passwords.
How does Web Drop beam passwords to my PC without internet? ▼
Web Drop uses Web Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) directly between your phone and your desktop browser. Bluetooth is localized radio communication, not the internet. The transmission is protected by ephemeral ECDH P-256 key agreement, client-side Proof of Work, and a matching 6-digit visual code displayed on both screens. Data exists only in computer RAM and is shredded after 30 seconds.
Can Kunjika replace Google Authenticator? ▼
Yes! Kunjika features a built-in RFC 6238 TOTP authenticator engine. When creating or editing any credential, simply paste your 2FA secret key. Kunjika displays live 6-digit rotating codes with animated 30-second countdown rings.
Why should I care that INTERNET permission is missing? ▼
When an Android app does not declare android.permission.INTERNET in its manifest, the Android operating system kernel denies network socket creation. By eliminating internet connectivity from the app entirely, Kunjika removes remote network attack surfaces and prevents cloud data harvesting. Outbound data transfer occurs strictly when you explicitly initiate it via local, biometric-authenticated channels (Direct Web Drop over Bluetooth or Encrypted QR sync).
Is Kunjika completely free? ▼
Yes. Kunjika is 100% free and open-source software licensed under the MIT License. There are no subscriptions, no ads, no trackers, and no premium paywalls.

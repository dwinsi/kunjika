Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Kunjika's security, offline model, and setup.

If Kunjika is 100% offline, what happens if I lose or break my phone? ▼
Because Kunjika does not store your vault on cloud servers, nobody else holds a copy of your passwords. That is why Kunjika includes two easy sovereign backup tools in Settings:

1. Emergency Recovery Kit: Generates an encrypted, printable physical backup document you can store in your home safe or deposit box.
2. Encrypted Vault Export: Export your double-encrypted database file to an external USB flash drive or physical cold storage.
How does Web Drop beam passwords to my PC without internet? ▼
Web Drop uses Web Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) directly between your phone and your desktop browser. Bluetooth is localized radio communication, not the internet. The transmission is protected by ephemeral ECDH P-256 key agreement, client-side Proof of Work, and a matching 6-digit visual code displayed on both screens. Data exists only in computer RAM and is shredded after 30 seconds.
Is my Master PIN stored on the phone? ▼
No plaintext Master PIN is ever stored anywhere. Your PIN is encrypted directly inside the phone's physical security chip (Android KeyStore TEE / StrongBox). When you touch your fingerprint, the hardware chip securely unwraps the key without exposing plaintext in storage or memory.
Can Kunjika replace Google Authenticator? ▼
Yes! Kunjika features a built-in RFC 6238 TOTP authenticator engine. When creating or editing any credential, simply paste your 2FA secret key. Kunjika displays live 6-digit rotating codes with animated 30-second countdown rings.
Why should I care that INTERNET permission is missing? ▼
When an Android app does not declare android.permission.INTERNET in its manifest, the Android operating system kernel denies network socket creation. By eliminating internet connectivity from the app entirely, Kunjika removes remote network attack surfaces and prevents cloud data harvesting.
Is Kunjika completely free? ▼
Yes. Kunjika is 100% free and open-source software licensed under the MIT License. There are no subscriptions, no ads, no trackers, and no premium paywalls.

🎯 Bite-Sized Testing Missions

Follow these quick, easy tasks spread across your 14-day journey:

🚀
Days 1 – 2 First Launch & Biometric Setup
  • Download Kunjika from the Google Play Closed Beta opt-in link.
  • Swipe through onboarding and choose a memorable 4 to 8-digit Master PIN.
  • Tap Link Biometrics to enable instant Fingerprint or Face unlock.
  • Lock and unlock the app 2–3 times to verify fast unlocking.
🔄
Days 3 – 5 Password Generator & Vault Storage
  • Open the Generator tab and try generating: a Random Password, a multi-word Passphrase (like correct-horse-battery-staple), and a numeric PIN.
  • Watch the real-time strength meter and estimated crack time change.
  • Save 2–3 sample logins in the Vault under categories (Personal, Work, Finance).
  • Search for a saved login using the top search bar.
⏰
Days 6 – 8 2FA Authenticator & Android Autofill
  • Add a 2FA secret key to one of your accounts and watch the rotating 6-digit code and 30s countdown timer (replaces Google Authenticator!).
  • Go to Settings > Autofill Service and enable Kunjika as your Android default.
  • Open any website login form in Chrome: tap the password box and let Kunjika autofill with one fingerprint tap.
⚡
Days 9 – 11 Beam Passwords to PC ("Web Drop")
  • On your laptop (Chrome, Edge, or Brave), open dwinsi.github.io/kunjika/web-companion/.
  • On your phone, open any saved vault item and tap ⚡ Web Drop.
  • Scan the QR code on your computer screen and confirm the matching 6-digit pairing code.
  • Touch your fingerprint: the password automatically copies to your laptop clipboard with zero cables, zero Wi-Fi, and zero cloud!
🛡️
Days 12 – 13 Security Check & Theme Customization
  • Open the Security tab: check your password hygiene score (weak or reused passwords) and phone attestation.
  • Go to Settings and toggle between the Obsidian Gold and Platinum Silver themes.
  • Tap Emergency Recovery Kit to view your printable offline recovery sheet.
💬
Day 14+ Submit Play Store Feedback & Stay Installed
  • Open Kunjika in the Google Play Store and write a quick private review.
  • Keep Kunjika installed on your phone until the developer announces official public launch!

❓ Quick FAQ for Testers

Everything you might wonder about the 14-day test program:

How much time do I need to spend testing each day? +
Just 1 to 2 minutes every day or two! You don't have to spend hours in the app. Simply opening it, generating a random password, viewing an account, or testing autofill creates the active usage signals Google looks for.
Why can't I uninstall the app immediately on Day 14? +
After Day 14, the developer submits the application to Google Play for production approval. This review usually takes 2 to 5 business days. If testers uninstall while the review is pending, the active tester count drops below 20, which can pause or cancel the approval. Please keep Kunjika installed until public launch!
Are my passwords safe while testing Kunjika? +
100% safe. Kunjika doesn't even have internet permission in its code—meaning it is physically impossible for the app to send your data anywhere. All credentials are encrypted inside your phone's physical hardware security chip (Android KeyStore TEE).
What should I do if I find a bug or glitch? +
Please mention it directly in your Google Play Store private feedback, or report it on our GitHub repository. Include your phone model and Android version so we can investigate right away!
